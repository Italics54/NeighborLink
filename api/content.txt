NeighborLink is a community hub website built for neighbors and by neighbors. 
The goal of NeighborLink is to unify neighbors within a community, allowing them to interact, share information, and stay updated on what is happening in their neighborhood.

Website Structure:

1. **Home Page**  
   - The first page you see when visiting NeighborLink.  
   - To access community features, you must create an account or log in.  
   - Use the "Join" or "Sign In" button in the header. Enter your email, password, and select your community.

2. **Community Resources Page**  
   - Accessible after logging in.  
   - Displays numerous resources available within your community.  
   - You can monitor events, updates, and other community activity.

3. **Stories Page**  
   - Shows local stories posted by neighbors.  
   - You can read stories, leave comments, and engage with events and experiences in your community.

4. **Calendar Page**  
   - Displays the community calendar.  
   - Shows events, activities, and important dates happening in your neighborhood.  
   - Helps you plan participation or visit events.

5. **Submit a Resource Page**  
   - Allows you to submit a resource that may not be known to others in your community.  
   - Accessible via the header under your account dropdown.  
   - Fill out a few questions to submit a resource; submissions are reviewed by the NeighborLink team.

6. **Account Settings**  
   - Located at the top right of the page.  
   - Change the community you belong to.  
   - Access the submit resource button and other account options.  
   - To log out, click your account in the top right and select "Log Out." Logging out removes access to community features until you log in again.
